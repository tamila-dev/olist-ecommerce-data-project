Average Payment per Order

The highest average payment per order was recorded in September 2018 (277.47).

The lowest average payment per order was recorded in December 2016 (19.62).

In November 2017, the average payment per order was 158.39.

In April 2018, the average payment per order increased to 167.28, despite a lower number of orders compared to November 2017.

September 2018 had only 16 orders, so its average payment value should be interpreted with caution.

Note: Average payment per order is calculated by first aggregating payment values at the order level and then calculating the monthly average.
